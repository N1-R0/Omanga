(2026-10-28), AnswerThePublic "visiting nigeria" (GB, free tier), Search
Console. **No search volumes or difficulty scores**: no paid tool was
available, and ATP's free tier hides volumes. Current visibility for every
non-branded term below is zero impressions in GSC.

What Omanga can honestly satisfy: a multi-currency wallet funded in USD, GBP
or CAD, conversion to naira, paying by transfer in Nigeria (and 50+ African
countries); health cover in Nigeria only, from Phillips HMO. It is **not**
outbound travel insurance, not Africa-wide cover, and not a remittance
service in the Remitly sense. That rules out several brief hypotheses
("travel insurance for Africa", "emergency medical coverage for travel to
Africa"): the product does not serve them.
